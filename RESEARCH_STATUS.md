## Phase 10D adversarial audit — 11 November 2026

**Status: major scientific revision; no new force or faster-than-light signal has been observed.** An adversarial review found that the original Phase 10B exact-model synthetic validation did not test model misspecification. A new 3,000-trial-per-case simulation found 4.9% false positives under the matched null, 7.7% for correlated synthetic noise, and severe false detections for an unmodeled +10 μm gap shift in the **invented toy apparatus** (not in real Eöt-Wash data). The 0.5 injected signal in the original code is genuine; a paired-noise check recovers the expected shift.

A **conditional shared-likelihood** recast from earlier transcriptions of published *marginal single-Yukawa* fit summaries suggests the illustrative gravitational-strength two-pole benchmark (α=1) is in strong tension with existing data. **It is not an official or valid 95%-confidence experimental exclusion without the same joint nuisance model, apparatus responses, and cross-template covariance.** The static two-pole momentum decomposition does **not by itself** prove an Ostrogradsky ghost: the postulated model has higher **spatial**, not higher time, derivatives; a full physical theory is still unestablished.
