- Reproducibility of dispersive wave calculations and source–receiver responses under assumed equations.
- Numerical checks of static-force Green functions and finite-body idealizations.
- Secondary analysis of selected published torsion-balance measurements (Phase 9).

## Not established

- Faster-than-light information transfer or physical transport.

- Independent lab replication, peer review or operational propulsion.

## Next research gate
Reconstruct the full apparatus response for 95 measurement runs, including geometry, calibration, nuisance parameters, and covariance; reproduce the Newtonian fit, then test the two-pole prediction within the **same** likelihood. Publish null results and failures.
