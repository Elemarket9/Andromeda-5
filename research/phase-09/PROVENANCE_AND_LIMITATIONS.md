# Provenance and limitations

- The uploaded ANDROMEDA-5 Phase 8 hypothesis supplies the speculative two-pole kernel; not experimentally observed.
- The 95×3 torque values originate in Lee's University of Washington 2020 dissertation Appendix A.1 printed pp. 147–150, which publishes *corrected* experimental measurements. The transcription used text recovered from the online PDF, NOT original `.sum` experiment files. Its rows have automated consistency checks but have not been manually visually certified against every original printed table row.
- Published single-Yukawa profile values come from the same thesis Table 11.4 (printed pp. 134–136). They are NOT independent tests and do not give a joint likelihood for a two-pole potential.
- The author's published Newtonian p-value was independently computed from the published chi-square and degrees of freedom, not by independently computing Newtonian torque predictions.
- Some Appendix A values are below the headline 52 µm minimum stated in the PRL paper; this requires resolution by careful source reading or author clarification.
- Conditional correlation sweeps insert unknown overlaps and assume a Gaussian linearized joint model; this is for sensitivity inspection only, not a measured exclusion.
- To test a two-pole model properly, reproduce the experiment's Fourier-Bessel torque calculation (including composition if electron-coupled, geometry, calibration, systematic model and nuisance constraints) using the corrected measured data and full covariance, then test frequentist coverage / robustness.
- No new field, force, particle, or faster-than-light information transfer has been measured.
