# Phase 10 provenance and integrity

- Simulation inputs: selected mathematical benchmark ell=0.0002 m, mu=1000 m^-1 and synthetic point-mass ring geometry. Not measurements of the physical vacuum or any actual instrument.
- Numerical results: Python calculations executed in the research working environment on 11 November 2026.
- Third-party measurements: University of Washington J. G. Lee (2020) dissertation Appendix A.1 — processed, calibrated torques, not raw time-series data. Original authors must be cited, and data should be cross-checked against the PDF's page images.
- Local repetition / local fit: a diagnostic check, not a full Newtonian laboratory fit, and not an ANDROMEDA-5 measurement.
- No real two-pole exclusion limit has been calculated; the synthetic method cannot generate one.
- This is not peer-reviewed or professionally endorsed.
