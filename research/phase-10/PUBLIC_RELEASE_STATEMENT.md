# ANDROMEDA-5 Phase 10 — public wording

I have published Phase 10 of ANDROMEDA-5, an independent theoretical-physics project. We tested a hypothetical two-component short-range force with analytic/numeric torque comparisons and synthetic signal injection. The calculations are reproducible, but **no new force, faster-than-light signal, or violation of relativity has been detected**. I welcome university researchers' criticism of the matter coupling, modeling assumptions, and experimental test design. Full methods and code: https://github.com/Elemarket9/Andromeda-5/tree/main/research/phase-10
