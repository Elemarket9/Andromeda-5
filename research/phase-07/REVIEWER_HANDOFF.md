# Request for independent Phase 7 review

We seek a critical, independent review of a **hypothetical preferred-frame scalar with quartic spatial dispersion**. Its 3D static response is the difference of two Yukawa kernels. The code in this package computes finite-sphere force and infinite-slab pressure examples and reproduces the public 2020 Eot-Wash Newtonian chi-square p value.

**Questions for independent reviewers**

1. Is the EFT action well-posed, stable, and physically consistent when coupled to electrons? Are radiative corrections or preferred-frame constraints fatal?
2. Is the finite-body Yukawa form factor correctly applied to the two-pole Green function?
3. Which published short-range gravity dataset exposes raw torques, instrument geometry, covariance, and fitted nuisance parameters needed for a defensible 95% two-pole limit?
4. Does the chosen model predict any calibrated experiment capable of detecting the field beyond an arbitrary simulation? What sensitivity would be necessary?
5. What independent, falsifiable test would distinguish an exotic wave from photonic leakage or conventional electronic cross-talk?

**Important:** The reported published 38.6 um 95% range constraint is for a *single Yukawa interaction* with gravitational strength. It has NOT been recast to this model. No anomalous force or faster-than-light signal has been measured.
