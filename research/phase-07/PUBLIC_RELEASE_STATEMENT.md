# ANDROMEDA-5 Phase 7 — Public research status

11 November 2026

Our independent numerical investigation derives a static-force prediction for a **hypothetical** scalar field with a preferred-frame quartic dispersion relationship. We used existing published gravity tests as external constraints, reproduced a published Newtonian-fit probability, and calculated the predicted force for finite-sized idealized test bodies.

**No new force, faster-than-light signal, or new particle has been observed.** The available published summary does not suffice to assign a valid 95% experimental exclusion to our exact two-pole model. The research package includes code, mathematical assumptions, plots and a list of missing data required for an actual experimental reanalysis. We invite technically critical, independent review.
