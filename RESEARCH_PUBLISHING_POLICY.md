# Research publishing protocol — ANDROMEDA-5

**Standing project instruction:** When new ANDROMEDA-5 research is completed in an active ChatGPT session and the connected GitHub integration is available, commit necessary research artifacts directly to this repository's `main` branch and verify the resulting files. Do not claim any automatic background syncing or publishing outside an active session.

## Publish by default
- Complete reproducible scripts, tests, environment requirements and exact execution commands.
- Derived results, CSV data, relevant graphs, provenance notes and mathematical derivations.
- Corrected or newly completed research reports, with experimental evidence clearly separated from synthetic data, numerical models and speculative hypotheses.
- An updated research status and an explanation of unresolved blockers or failed tests.
- Bibliographic links or sufficiently attributed third-party measurements. Preserve original source rights.

## Do not publish automatically
- Secrets, credentials, personal correspondence, unpublished sensitive data or third-party personal information.
- Large redundant intermediate outputs or temporary/generated build caches.
- Unchecked conclusions described as discoveries, especially claims of faster-than-light signaling.
- Third-party materials lacking rights for republication.

## Verification gate
1. Identify and review files for accuracy and privacy.
2. Run available reproducibility tests (or explicitly state when they were not run).
3. Upload source, associated datasets, and report together when feasible.
4. Read back the committed tree or file(s) from GitHub to confirm publication.
5. Report actual paths, commit hashes, unresolved issues and any unpublished files.

**Scientific status:** No physical faster-than-light communication, new scalar field, novel fifth force, or controllable inertial-mass reduction has been demonstrated by ANDROMEDA-5. Software simulations are not experimental confirmation.
